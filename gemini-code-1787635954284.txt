================================================================================
          BRAND NAMING & CONFLICT ANALYSIS REPORT: QUICK-COMMERCE
================================================================================

PROJECT SCOPE: Quick-Commerce Delivery App (10–20 minute delivery)
TARGET MARKET: Kolar Gold Fields (KGF), Karnataka, India
LINGUISTIC FIT: Multilingual (Kannada, Telugu, Tamil, English)
NAMING FORMULA: Short (4–7 letters), punchy, ending with "-it" (like Blinkit)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. EVALUATED CANDIDATES & CONFLICT ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. SNAPIT
   - Score: 4/10
   - Strengths: High brand recall, fast-paced imagery, natural phrasing.
   - Conflicts: Direct competitor conflict. Existing Indian 10-min grocery delivery 
     app on Google Play ("Snapit" by Zepzen Technologies). Multiple Class 28 
     trademark records.
   - Verdict: AVOID (High legal & brand collision risk)

2. ZIPPY
   - Score: 3/10
   - Strengths: Energetic, easy to pronounce.
   - Conflicts: Direct Karnataka competitor. Existing quick-commerce grocery 
     service operating under "Zippy" in Tarikere, Karnataka. Active Class 35 
     retail/food trademark filings.
   - Verdict: AVOID (Regional confusion & legal barrier)

3. ZIKIT
   - Score: 5/10
   - Strengths: Trendy modern startup feel, memorable "Z" prefix.
   - Conflicts: Pharma conflict in India (registered antibiotic injection line); 
     international GPS tracking app; active AI service.
   - Verdict: AVOID (Search clutter & pharma trademark risks)

4. QUICKIT
   - Score: 3/10
   - Strengths: Immediately explains the service.
   - Conflicts: Direct duplicate. "Quickit Grocery" already exists on both the 
     Indian Google Play Store and Apple App Store. Objected/contested Indian 
     trademark filings.
   - Verdict: AVOID (Zero differentiation, app store rejection risk)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. STRONGEST ALTERNATIVE CONCEPTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   * BEGIT   -> From Kannada "Bega" (Fast) + it
   * SIGIT   -> From Kannada "Sigu" (To get / find) + it
   * BEKIT   -> From Kannada "Beku" (Need / want) + it
   * VANGIT  -> From Tamil "Vaangu" (To buy / receive) + it
   * DORAKIT -> From Telugu "Dorike / Dorakadam" (Found / available) + it

B. ORIGINAL ABSTRACT BLENDS
   * ZUVIT / ZORIT / ZEVIT -> Modern tech feel with high distinctiveness
   * TAKIT / TOKIT / TAVIT -> South Indian phonetic rhythm, short & punchy
   * VELIT                 -> Inspired by "Vega" (Speed)
