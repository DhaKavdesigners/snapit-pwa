# SnapIt - Order Status and Delivery Flow

PURPOSE
-------
Each status represents a real-world event and clearly identifies who is responsible
for triggering that status.

The main idea of the new flow is to add:

    OUT_OF_SHOP

between:

    READY_FOR_PICKUP
            and
    OUT_FOR_DELIVERY

This separates two different events:

    Merchant: "I have handed the order to the rider."
    Rider:    "I confirm that I received the order."

Only after both confirmations should the order become OUT_FOR_DELIVERY.


1. COMPLETE ORDER FLOW
----------------------

    CUSTOMER PLACES ORDER
            |
            v
         PLACED
            |
            | Merchant: Accept & Pack
            v
        PREPARING
            |
            | Rider is assigned
            v
    Merchant prepares order
            |
            | Merchant: Mark Packed & Ready
            v
    READY_FOR_PICKUP
            |
            | Rider is notified
            v
    Rider goes to the shop
            |
            | Merchant: Hand Over to Delivery Rider
            v
        OUT_OF_SHOP
            |
            | Rider: Confirm Order Received
            v
    OUT_FOR_DELIVERY
            |
            | Rider: Mark as Delivered
            v
        DELIVERED


2. PLACED
---------

Meaning:
    The customer has successfully placed the order.

What happens:
    - Customer submits the order.
    - System creates the order.
    - Merchant receives the new order.
    - Order waits for the merchant to accept it.
    - Rider is not required at this stage.

Merchant can see:
    - Order ID
    - Items ordered
    - Quantity
    - Customer details
    - Delivery address
    - Order amount
    - Special instructions

Merchant action:
    ACCEPT & PACK

Transition:
    PLACED -> PREPARING


3. PREPARING
------------

Meaning:
    The merchant has accepted the order and has started preparing it.

What happens:
    - Merchant starts preparing the order.
    - System starts rider assignment.
    - A suitable rider is assigned.
    - Rider receives the shop/location information.
    - Rider can travel towards the shop.

Important:
    The rider does not need to enter the shop immediately.
    The rider can wait outside while the order is being prepared.

Merchant responsibility:
    Prepare and pack the complete order.

Rider responsibility:
    Travel towards the shop and wait until the order is ready.

Transition:
    PREPARING -> READY_FOR_PICKUP


4. READY_FOR_PICKUP
-------------------

Meaning:
    The order is completely prepared, packed, and ready to be collected
    by the assigned rider.

What happens:
    - Merchant has finished preparing the order.
    - Merchant marks the order as ready.
    - Assigned rider receives a notification.
    - Rider can now enter the shop and collect the order.

Merchant action:
    MARK PACKED & READY

Example rider notification:

    Order Ready for Pickup
    Order #1234 is ready for pickup at the store.

Transition:
    READY_FOR_PICKUP -> OUT_OF_SHOP

This transition happens when the merchant physically hands the order to
the rider and confirms the handover.


5. OUT_OF_SHOP
--------------

Meaning:
    The merchant has confirmed that the order has been physically handed
    over to the rider.

Important:
    OUT_OF_SHOP does NOT mean that the rider has already confirmed receipt.

At this point:

    Merchant:
        "I have handed over the order."

    Rider:
        Still needs to confirm:
        "I received the order."

Merchant action:
    HAND OVER TO DELIVERY RIDER

After the merchant clicks the action:

    READY_FOR_PICKUP -> OUT_OF_SHOP

Rider notification:

    Order Handed Over
    The merchant has handed over this order to you.
    Please confirm that you have received the order.

    [ CONFIRM ORDER RECEIVED ]


6. RIDER CONFIRMS ORDER RECEIVED
--------------------------------

The rider checks that the order has been physically received.

Rider action:
    CONFIRM ORDER RECEIVED

Only after the rider confirms should the order move forward.

Transition:
    OUT_OF_SHOP -> OUT_FOR_DELIVERY

This transition is triggered by the rider.


7. OUT_FOR_DELIVERY
-------------------

Meaning:
    The rider has confirmed that they have the order and is now responsible
    for delivering it to the customer.

At this point:
    - Rider has possession of the order.
    - Delivery process has officially started.
    - Customer can be informed that the order is out for delivery.

Rider should be able to see:
    - Customer name
    - Customer phone/contact option
    - Delivery address
    - Navigation
    - Order details
    - Delivery instructions

Rider action:
    MARK AS DELIVERED

Transition:
    OUT_FOR_DELIVERY -> DELIVERED


8. DELIVERED
------------

Meaning:
    The rider has successfully delivered the order to the customer.

What happens:
    - Customer receives the order.
    - Rider's delivery task is completed.
    - Order is no longer an active delivery.
    - Merchant can see that the order was delivered.
    - Post-delivery processes can be triggered.

This is the final delivery status.


9. COMPLETE STATE MACHINE
-------------------------

    +---------+
    | PLACED  |
    +----+----+
         |
         | Merchant: Accept & Pack
         v
    +------------+
    | PREPARING  |
    +-----+------+
          |
          | Merchant: Mark Packed & Ready
          v
    +------------------+
    | READY_FOR_PICKUP |
    +--------+---------+
             |
             | Merchant: Hand Over to Delivery Rider
             v
    +--------------+
    | OUT_OF_SHOP  |
    +------+-------+
           |
           | Rider: Confirm Order Received
           v
    +------------------+
    | OUT_FOR_DELIVERY |
    +--------+---------+
             |
             | Rider: Mark as Delivered
             v
    +-----------+
    | DELIVERED |
    +-----------+


10. STATUS AND RESPONSIBILITY
-----------------------------

Use the following as the final responsibility mapping:

    STATUS              MEANING                                  TRIGGERED BY
    ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
    PLACED              Customer placed the order                Customer/System
    PREPARING           Merchant accepted and is preparing       Merchant
    READY_FOR_PICKUP    Order is fully packed and ready          Merchant
    OUT_OF_SHOP         Merchant handed order to rider            Merchant
    OUT_FOR_DELIVERY    Rider confirmed receiving the order       Rider
    DELIVERED           Rider delivered the order                Rider
    ---------------------------------------------------------------------------


11. RESPONSIBILITY BY USER
--------------------------

CUSTOMER
--------
    Place Order
        |
        v
    PLACED


MERCHANT
--------
    Accept & Pack
        |
        v
    PREPARING

    Mark Packed & Ready
        |
        v
    READY_FOR_PICKUP

    Hand Over to Delivery Rider
        |
        v
    OUT_OF_SHOP


RIDER
-----
    Confirm Order Received
        |
        v
    OUT_FOR_DELIVERY

    Mark as Delivered
        |
        v
    DELIVERED


12. WHY OUT_OF_SHOP IS NEEDED
-----------------------------

Without OUT_OF_SHOP, the system could incorrectly assume:

    Merchant:
        "I handed over the order."
                |
                v
        "Rider has the order."

These are actually two separate confirmations.

The new flow makes them explicit:

    MERCHANT
    "I handed over the order."
            |
            v
       OUT_OF_SHOP
            |
            v
    RIDER
    "I confirm that I received the order."
            |
            v
    OUT_FOR_DELIVERY


13. IMPORTANT BUSINESS RULE
---------------------------

The system must NOT automatically change:

    OUT_OF_SHOP -> OUT_FOR_DELIVERY

just because the merchant has handed over the order.

The rider must explicitly confirm receipt.

Therefore:

    OUT_OF_SHOP
         |
         v
    Rider receives confirmation request
         |
         +----------------------+
         |                      |
         | Confirmed             | Not confirmed
         v                      v
    OUT_FOR_DELIVERY        Remains OUT_OF_SHOP


14. EDGE CASE - RIDER DOES NOT CONFIRM
--------------------------------------

If the rider does not confirm the order:

    Current status remains:
        OUT_OF_SHOP

The system should NOT automatically assume that the rider has received
the order.

A timeout or escalation mechanism can be added later.

Example:

    OUT_OF_SHOP
         |
         v
    Rider does not confirm
         |
         v
    System waits / alerts
         |
         v
    Possible escalation to support or operations


15. FINAL RECOMMENDED FLOW
--------------------------

    PLACED
       |
       v
    PREPARING
       |
       v
    READY_FOR_PICKUP
       |
       v
    OUT_OF_SHOP
       |
       v
    OUT_FOR_DELIVERY
       |
       v
    DELIVERED


KEY HANDOVER RULE
-----------------

    READY_FOR_PICKUP
            |
            | Merchant hands order to rider
            v
       OUT_OF_SHOP
            |
            | Rider confirms receipt
            v
     OUT_FOR_DELIVERY


FINAL DEFINITIONS
-----------------

    OUT_OF_SHOP
        = Merchant has confirmed the order was handed to the rider.

    OUT_FOR_DELIVERY
        = Rider has confirmed receipt and has possession of the order.

Therefore the complete responsibility chain is:

    CUSTOMER
       |
       v
    MERCHANT
       |
       v
    RIDER
       |
       v
    CUSTOMER


FINAL STATUS SEQUENCE
---------------------

    PLACED
      ->
    PREPARING
      ->
    READY_FOR_PICKUP
      ->
    OUT_OF_SHOP
      ->
    OUT_FOR_DELIVERY
      ->
    DELIVERED
